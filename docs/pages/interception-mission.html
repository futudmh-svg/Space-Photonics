📚 References & Data Sources

All quantitative values on this page were validated against published data (see Space Photonics research brief). Key anchors:

  • Starlink v1.5/v2 optical ISL: 100 Gbps per link, max 5,400 km (SpaceX/SPIE Photonics West, Jan 2024); SDA-class terminals (Tesat SCOT80, Mynaric CONDOR Mk3) 2.5–10 Gbps.
  • TBIRD (MIT LL/NASA): 200 Gbps LEO→ground downlink demonstrated, 1550 nm coherent, ~1 W EDFA (SPIE 12413, 2023; CLEO 2025). Optical link budgets close with single-digit-to-~10 dB margin after pointing/atmospheric losses.
  • Space-based HGV tracking (HBTSS / SDA Tracking Layer): performance classified ("fire-control-quality"); published multi-satellite geolocation literature gives ~25–500 m track errors (MDPI Appl. Sci. 11(13):6079, 2021). This page uses 25–250 m class.
  • Plasma blackout: f_p ≈ 8980·√N_e [Hz, N_e in cm⁻³]; Mach 10 @ 30 km → N_e ~10¹¹–10¹² cm⁻³ → f_p ≈ 3–9+ GHz (RAM-C flight data, NASA NTRS 19710004000). X-band blocked; Ka/EHF survive; optical (193 THz) immune.
  • NASA ILLUMA-T: 1.2 Gbps ISS↔LCRD optical relay (2023–24).
  • Thin-film lithium tantalate SHG 1550↔775 nm, 229 %/W/cm² (Qi et al., arXiv:2505.03162, 2025).
  • P_kill: modeled SSPK 0.7–0.9 (APS/38 North literature); 3-shot salvo → 1−(1−0.7)³ ≈ 0.97. No operational hypersonic-intercept Pk exists — shown as modeled.
