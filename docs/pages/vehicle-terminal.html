🥉 Concept C: Fiber-Optic Feedthrough

Minimal protrusion but limited power handling and pointing.

Fiber typeSingle-mode, gold-coated

TerminationFlush with skin, lens-coupled

Limitations

No beam steering — requires vehicle to point at satellite. Power limited to ~100 mW (fiber damage threshold). Cannot support high-bandwidth downlink.

Sapphire Window Thermal Analysis

The window must survive a 2000°C surface while keeping internal optics at 25°C.

Steady-State Heat Conduction
Q = k × A × (T_hot - T_cold) / d

k = 35 W/m·K (sapphire)
A = π × (25 mm)² = 1.96 × 10⁻³ m²
T_hot = 2000°C, T_cold = 25°C
d = 15 mm = 0.015 m

Q = 35 × 1.96×10⁻³ × (1975) / 0.015 = 9.0 kW

This is enormous. Solutions:
1. Transpiration cooling (bleed air through porous window)
2. Ablative coating (sacrificial layer, replaces each flight)
3. Reduced window size (25 mm → 15 mm, Q drops to 3.2 kW)

Transpiration Cooling Concept

Inject coolant (liquid nitrogen or water) through porous sapphire. Coolant evaporates at surface, creating protective film.

Coolant mass flow: ṁ = Q / h_fg
For water: h_fg = 2260 kJ/kg
ṁ = 9000 / 2.26×10⁶ = 4 g/s

For 300 s flight: 1.2 kg water required.
This is feasible for a 1000+ kg vehicle.

Line-of-Sight Kinematics

Vehicle velocity: v = 3430 m/s (Mach 10)
Satellite angular rate: ω = v / R = 3430 / 300000 = 11.4 mrad/s = 0.65°/s

Required track bandwidth: f_3dB > 5 × ω_max / θ_accuracy
= 5 × 0.65°/s / 0.006° = 542 Hz

FSM must update at > 1 kHz with < 100 μrad resolution.
