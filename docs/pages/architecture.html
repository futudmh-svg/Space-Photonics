🔷 PPLN Cascaded χ⁽²⁾ Nonlinearity

Sum-Frequency Generation (SFG) creates an effective giant third-order nonlinearity. Signal (1550 nm) + Pump (1560 nm) → Idler (777.5 nm). The back-conversion imprints pump-dependent phase shift on the signal.
