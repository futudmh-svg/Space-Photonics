Space Photonics Mesh Network — Orbital Photonics Fabric (OPF) v3.0

System Overview

The Orbital Photonics Fabric (OPF) is a photonic mesh network in Very Low Earth Orbit (VLEO). It provides:

  • 10 Gbps inter-satellite links (ISL) with 1 ms latency (near-term target; 100+ Gbps research)
  • Hypersonic vehicle tracking (Mach 5-10) at 10 Hz update rate
  • Two-way communication with hypersonic platforms
  • Mesh networking: 150+ satellites, self-healing, dynamic routing
  • EHF backup (100 GHz) for cloud cover and plasma blackout

EHF Backup Architecture

EHF (100 GHz) provides backup for cloud cover and plasma blackout scenarios where optical fails.

ParameterValue
Frequency100 GHz (W-band)
Transmit power10 W
Antenna30 cm dish (sat), 30 cm (vehicle)
Antenna gain48 dBi
Free space loss182 dB @ 300 km
Atmospheric loss2 dB (clear), 10 dB (rain)
Plasma loss3-10 dB (n_e = 10¹⁸ m⁻³)
Received power-65 dBm
Receiver sensitivity-80 dBm
Link margin-15 dB (FAILS at peak plasma)
Data rate1 Gbps
⚠️ EHF Limitation: At peak plasma density (n_e = 10¹⁹ m⁻³), f_p = 28 GHz. EHF at 100 GHz has f/f_p = 3.6, resulting in 10+ dB additional loss. EHF cannot reliably penetrate peak plasma. Optical is the only reliable link during cruise phase.

Key Design Decisions

DecisionRationaleTrade-off
1550 nm (not 850 nm)Eye-safe, low atmospheric loss, mature telecom componentsLarger aperture for same gain
OPA (not gimbal)No moving parts, μs-scale switching, 6-face coverageLimited FOV (±15°), lower gain than dish
TFLN (not Si)Lower loss, higher power handling, better EO coefficientLarger footprint, harder to integrate
PPLN (not APD only)Photon-limited-class sensitivity, 780 nm → 1550 nm conversionRequires pump laser, thermal control
300 km orbit (not 550 km)Lower latency (1 ms vs 2 ms), smaller link budget, drag-compensatedHigher drag, needs propulsion
EHF backup (not Ka-band)Better plasma penetration, smaller antennaHigher atmospheric loss, less mature
