OPA Goals — Maximum Distance, Steering Speed, Beamwidth

Photon-1 flies a hybrid architecture: a chip-scale optical phased array as an inertia-free fine steerer behind a discrete 5 cm telescope aperture. A pure 5 cm lobe-free OPA would need ≈ 4.2×10⁹ elements at λ/2 pitch — the hybrid gets the same 100 dBi-class gain with a mm-scale 512–4096-element chip.

⚠ DESIGN RULE — OPA chip for lobe-free µs steering · discrete 5 cm telescope for gain. Never stretch chip pitch to fake aperture.

04 · Hypersonic Tracking — Beam on a Mach-10 Glider

An HGV at Mach 10 (~3.4 km/s) crossing a 300 km slant range demands only 0.65 °/s — but with µrad-class beam discipline. The OPA's inertia-free µs stepping time-shares one aperture between the HGV track and a 10 Gbps ISL, feeding the site's multi-terminal triangulation concept: two+ terminals angle-track the same glider to fix its 3D state.
