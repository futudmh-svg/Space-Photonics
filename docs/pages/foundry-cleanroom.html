All-optical 12U photonic CubeSat, built on one line.

Photon-1 is a free-space optical communications terminal integrated on a 12U bus. Every unit flows through a single ISO-classified line: SiN waveguide fabrication, thin-film lithium niobate poling, optical phased array assembly, and environmental qualification — with yield gated at every step.
