**Mission:** Detect, track, triangulate adversary HGV using 3+ constellation satellites

**Key Math:**
- Plasma frequency: f_p = (1/2π) √(n_e × e² / m_e × ε₀) = **8.97 GHz** for n_e = 10¹⁸ m⁻³
- Optical at 193.5 THz is **21,500× above cutoff** → full penetration
- Angular precision: σ_α = θ_BW / (2√2 × SNR) = 0.055 μrad (55 nrad)
- Position accuracy: σ_x = (r²/B) × σ_α = **1 cm** for r=300 km, B=500 km
- Tracking loop: 1 kHz OPA update rate, 100 Hz bandwidth, Kalman filter

**State Machine:**
ACQUIRE → TRACK → TRIANGULATE → HANDOFF (all auto, no human in loop)

### Scenario B — Friendly Hypersonic Control (Two-Way Comms)

- Multi-beam: 4 simultaneous beams, −6 dB per beam, still **+30.5 dB margin**
