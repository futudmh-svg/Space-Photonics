## 2026-08-15 Evening Session — Full Technical Analysis & Research Cross-Reference

### Analysis Performed
Comprehensive technical audit of the entire 11-page Space Photonics web app against peer-reviewed literature and market research.

### Key Findings

**Contradictions Found:**
1. **OPA Scale (HIGH):** App claims 6-face × 256-element OPA (1536 elements). State-of-the-art is 32 channels on TFLN (2025). **48× gap** — requires fundamental breakthroughs, not just engineering.
2. **Photonic Tracking (HIGH):** App implies closed-loop optical tracking with photonic processing works. **Never demonstrated.** End-to-end latency, noise accumulation, radiation effects all unresolved.
3. **All-Optical Mesh (MEDIUM):** Presented as solved. In reality: SpaceX uses RF for inter-sat links; Mynaric terminals are point-to-point, not mesh. **Reconfigurable optical mesh in space is TRL 1-2.**
4. **Monolithic Foundry (MEDIUM):** Claims 3-material monolithic stack (TFLN + Si₃N₄ + Ag-chalcogenide). **No foundry offers this.** Only hybrid integration (die bonding) is available.

**Validated Claims:**
- TFLN fast switching: 12-14 ns (confirmed 2025)
- Si₃N₄ low-loss routing: <0.1 dB/m (well established)
- Chalcogenide Kerr nonlinearity: n₂ > 10⁻¹⁸ m²/W (confirmed 2025)
- Photonic MZI matrix multiplication: Demonstrated at 25 GBaud (confirmed 2021-2023)
- VLEO market growth: Falcon 9 at $2200/kg, Starship targeting $100/kg (confirmed 2026)
